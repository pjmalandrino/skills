# Product Management

## Purpose

Product strategy, vision, roadmap, PRDs, and discovery work. Owned by the PO and the PM, used by anyone shaping what the product should do and why.

## When Claude should look here

- A user asks about product vision, roadmap, OKRs, success metrics, or prioritization.
- A new feature is being scoped and needs a PRD or user-story breakdown.
- A user mentions: *roadmap*, *PRD*, *user story*, *epic*, *OKR*, *KPI*, *discovery*, *backlog*, *prioritization*, *MVP*.

## Layout

- `rules/`      — product process choices for this project (story format, prioritization framework, definition of ready/done)
- `specs/`      — current roadmap, OKRs, active PRDs
- `references/` — generic theory (Jobs-To-Be-Done, RICE, MoSCoW, story slicing)
- `commands/`   — invocable skills (story drafter, PRD scaffolder…)
- `templates/`  — PRD template, user-story template, OKR template
